### Building free tools that never upload your files

I build [**Cromagnon**](https://cromagnon.app): a free suite of PDF, image, spreadsheet, document, CSV and study tools that run entirely in your browser. Your files stay on your device.

- 🧰 10 studios and 70+ quick tools, free, with no sign-up or watermarks
- 🔒 Files are processed locally with JavaScript and WebAssembly, never uploaded
- ✈️ Most tools keep working offline after the first load
- 🌍 Made for everyone, everywhere

#### Open source

- [**awesome-no-upload-tools**](https://github.com/adil0day/awesome-no-upload-tools): a curated, CC0 list of free browser tools that keep your files private, Cromagnon's and other people's. Suggestions welcome.
